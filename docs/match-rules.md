# Match rules (v1)

These stay the same no matter which IP we lock. Fiction names change. Numbers should not, unless a playtest forces it.

## Lobby

There is no lobby in v1. Press play, the match starts. Bots are already on the pads.

## Roster

- 1 human
- 7 bots
- Names come from the IP list, not from players. The human's cart is labeled "You".

## Clock

- 180 seconds, counting down.
- The match ends at 0. No overtime in v1.
- Ties are ties. The board shows them. No sudden death until we have seen real ties and they feel bad.

## Driving

- Hold accelerate to reach top speed. Release to coast. Brake to stop faster and to sharpen a slide.
- Steer scales down at very low speed so you cannot spin in place like a turret.
- Slide: braking while turning, above a speed threshold, reduces grip for a short window.
- Carts bump. The lighter push wins a little. Nobody is a wall.
- Leave the arena floor and you respawn. You do not score for your own fall.

## Weapons

- One held weapon. Empty is allowed.
- Fire spends it. There is no ammo count beyond the one shot, except the slick, which drops a trail and is then gone.
- A new pickup replaces whatever you are holding. The old one is lost, not dropped.
- You cannot fire during spawn protection.

### Roles (fiction comes from the IP)

| Slot | Job | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Fast, straight, small push | The shot you use most |
| Heavy | Slower, arcing, big spin | The shot that knocks people out |
| Denial | A patch on the ground | Steals someone's line |

## Pickups

- Four on the arena at a time.
- First weapon in the match is random across the three.
- After that, the arena avoids giving you the same weapon three times in a row.
- A taken pickup returns after 4 seconds, somewhere else if its spot is occupied.

## Knockouts

- A hit applies a shove along the shot, plus a small spin.
- Heavy hits apply more of both.
- If you leave the floor within 3 seconds of being hit, the last cart that hit you scores 1.
- A second hit replaces who gets the credit.
- Spawn protection lasts 1.5 seconds. You cannot be shoved, and you cannot fire.
- Respawn faces the middle of the arena.

## Bots

- They use the same cart and the same weapons.
- They do not read your inputs.
- Their aim is offset by a few degrees, more when you are sliding.
- If a bot is stuck for a second, it reverses, then picks a new target.

## Results

Sort by knockouts, then by who got there first. Show all eight. Save the human's knockout count if it beats the number stored on this device.

## Not in v1

Teams, flags, hats, lives, shields, drifts that are their own meter, weapon combos, or anything that needs a second player.
