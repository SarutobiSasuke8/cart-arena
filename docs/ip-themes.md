# IP themes

Status: **unlocked.** Five original worlds for the same match. None of them use characters, maps, weapons, UI, or audio from Smash Karts or any other kart franchise. The loop we are borrowing is mechanical only: a closed arena, a cart, crates, knockouts, a short clock, bots.

Pick one as the base IP. The others can become later arenas if they still fit the handling. Do not blend them in v1. A mixed theme reads as "generic kart game."

## How to judge a theme

A good base IP for this build has:

- A **cart you understand in one second** from a chase camera.
- **Three weapons** that are knockouts, not guns from another genre.
- **One arena** that explains itself without a tutorial.
- A reason the match is **solo against bots** (a league, a night shift, a closed shop) so missing online play is fiction, not a missing feature.
- Shapes we can build from simple 3D geometry.

## 1. Gutter Prix — recommended

**Working title:** Gutter Prix
**League:** The Bench League
**One line:** After lights-out, the workbench is a stadium.

Caplings are bottle-cap people with clothespin bodies. They run modified gutter carts on a full-size workbench once the shop closes. Wheels are bottle caps. Suspension is a rubber band. The sport is not a race. It is who can flick everyone else off the bench.

**Arena 1 — The Bench.** Plywood deck. A vise and a toolbox are walls. Stacked books are a ramp. A spilled solvent puddle is a slide. A desk fan is a wind lane. A lazy susan in the middle spins you if you sit on it. Falling off the edge is the knockout.

**Driver silhouette:** cap helmet, two wire arms, no face franchise.

**Pickups:** upside-down coffee tins. Not question-mark boxes.

**Weapons for v1:**

| Weapon | What it does |
|---|---|
| Band Shot | Fast rubber band. Light knockback. The reliable tool. |
| Marble Mortar | Arcing marble. Splash spin if it lands near a cart. |
| Soap Slick | A trail. Whoever drives through it loses their line. |

**Voice:** a shop radio. Short lines. "Tin up." "Off the bench."

**Palette:** tungsten yellow, plywood tan, safety-tape yellow, one enamel-blue cart for the player. Bots are enamel red, green, white, and black so they read at a glance.

**Why this one:** the toy scale is the IP. Weapons are not firearms. A solo match makes sense (the shop is closed, the league is you and the other caps). It is the furthest from animal racers and item-box karts, and it is the easiest arena to read.

## 2. Veldt Post — Bay Brawl

**Company:** Veldt Post, invented. Not a real carrier.
**Sport:** Bay Brawl, unofficial, night shift only.
**One line:** Bay 4 stopped sorting parcels and started sorting each other.

Drivers wear high-vis with blank name patches. You stamp your own. Carts are pallet jacks cut down into sit-down racers.

**Arena 1 — Bay 4.** Sorting floor. A conveyor shoves you. Scanner gates are speed pads. Shrink-wrap pillars are cover. Chutes are the out-of-bounds.

**Weapons for v1:**

| Weapon | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tape Lash | A short sticky shot. Light knockback, a brief slow. |
| Parcel Mortar | A cardboard box in an arc. Heavy hit. |
| Label Gun | Tags a cart. They turn wider until the tag falls off. |

**Knockout fiction:** you get mis-sorted. A chute takes you and drops you back at your bay.

**Palette:** kraft brown, safety orange, scanner amber, concrete grey.

**Why you would pick it:** clean satire, very readable shapes, easy to add a second bay later. Weaker than Gutter Prix only because "warehouse kart" is a more familiar pitch.

## 3. Harborfry — Lantern Row

**City:** Harborfry, a fictional cold-water port. Not a real city and not a costume of one.
**Street:** Lantern Row, after the stalls close.
**One line:** The morning's best pitch is settled in the alley tonight.

Oilskin drivers. Stall carts. Food that exists only here: salt knots, lamp-oil buns. Coal fog, brass lanterns, salt stains. If the art slips into a generic night market, the IP fails. The cold port is the whole point.

**Arena 1 — The Row.** One closed alley. Stall counters are walls. A crate bridge is the high line. A gutter of meltwater is the slick.

**Weapons for v1:**

| Weapon | What it does |
|---|---|
| Chili Slick | A burning trail. Same job as soap, different fiction. |
| Wok Lid | A thrown disc that bounces once. |
| Steam Burst | A close blast of steam. Point-blank knockout. |

**Palette:** coal teal, brass amber, oilskin black, dough gold.

**Why you would pick it:** the strongest mood. Higher art risk. Weapons must stay specific or it becomes a food-item kart.

## 4. Cinder Shift — The Pour

**Place:** an invented foundry. Apprentices, not heroes.
**Sport:** The Pour. One heat, then the floor belongs to whoever is still upright.
**One line:** The slag carts were for moving metal. That lasted a week.

**Extra rule, only if this is the base IP:** a heat meter. Sit in the orange too long and the tires glaze — you cannot turn. Quench puddles clear the heat and kill your speed. This is a real system, so it should not be bolted onto another theme.

**Arena 1 — Cooling Floor.** Black plates, ember cracks, one quench puddle, a moving crucible that is a hazard, not a pickup.

**Weapons for v1:**

| Weapon | What it does |
|---|---|
| Spark Lance | A straight zap. Precise. |
| Slag Glob | Slow arcing blob. Big spin. |
| Quench Bomb | A burst that clears heat and shoves everyone nearby. |

**Palette:** black, ember orange, safety green.

**Why you would pick it:** the most "game system" of the five. Worse as a first build because the heat meter is a second lesson on top of driving and shooting.

## 5. Lot 19 — Claim

**Place:** a leased crater. The sport is staking a claim with utility buggies.
**One line:** Low gravity, short leases, bad neighbors.

Hop replaces drift. Tethers replace missiles. This does **not** feel like the arena kart we are modelling. Park it as a later experiment, not the base IP.

**Weapons, if we ever build it:** tether harpoon, regolith spray, beacon jam.

---

## Recommendation

Lock **Gutter Prix** for v1.

- The Bench is one screen of fiction.
- Three weapons map onto light hit, heavy hit, and area denial.
- Bots are other Caplings. No network is required for the fantasy.
- Veldt Post is the backup if you want satire over toys.
- Harborfry is the backup if you want mood over readability.
- Cinder Shift is a mode we can add after the match loop exists.
- Lot 19 is a different game.
